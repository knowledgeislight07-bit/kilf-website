About the course

Learn your Deen, then live it

The course helps students learn the Deen and understand why it matters to put it into practice. It covers the fundamentals of Islam and opens the door to advanced study such as Tafseer, Arabic grammar and in-depth Fiqh.

Students begin with core beliefs and worship and move year by year into Hadith, Arabic and classical Islamic texts, while balancing their studies with school, work and family.

Our approach

Five parts of a Muslim’s life

Every year of the course is built around these five fundamentals.

  1. إيمانياتImaaniyaatCore beliefs
  2. عبادات‘IbadaatWorship of Allah
  3. أخلاقAkhlaaqGood character
  4. معاملاتMu‘amalaatMoney and business dealings
  5. معاشراتMu‘asharaatDealings in society
