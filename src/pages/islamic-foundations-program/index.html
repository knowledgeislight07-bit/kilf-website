Islamic Foundations Program

Our four-year foundational Qur’an & Islamic Studies course — live, online, and built around school, work, and family.

About the course

Learn your Deen. Live your Deen.

Our foundational course helps students learn — and understand the importance of implementing — the Deen in their lives. It covers the fundamental aspects of Islam and opens the door to more advanced study such as Tafseer, Arabic grammar, and in-depth Fiqh.

The goal is a comprehensive curriculum of Qur’an and Islamic Studies that students can balance with school, work, and other responsibilities — beginning with foundational beliefs and worship and advancing year by year into Hadith, Arabic, and classical Islamic texts.

Our approach

Five components of a Muslim’s life

Every year of the course is structured around these five fundamentals:

  1. ImaaniyaatCore beliefs
  2. ‘IbadaatWorship of Allah
  3. AkhlaaqDeveloping good character
  4. Mu‘amalaatMonetary & business dealings
  5. Mu‘asharaatSocietal dealings
