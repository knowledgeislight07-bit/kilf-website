Year 3 subjects

What you’ll study

  • Qur’an and Tajweed

    Students are placed in a Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced Qur’an section based on their skill level.

  • Arabic Language

    Arabic grammar, vocabulary and how to form sentences.

  • Fiqh, part 3

    Students continue their study of Fiqh topics.

  • Adaabul Muta‘allimeen and Hayatus Sahabah

    The expectations of a student of knowledge, and the lives of those around the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).

  • Hadith

    Continuing Zad-ut-Talibeen by Shaykh Ashiq Ilahi Bulandshehri (RH), alongside selected chapters of Riyad-us-Saliheen.

  • 99 Names of Allah

    Memorizing the last 50 Names of Allah in Arabic and English.
