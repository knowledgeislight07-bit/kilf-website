Year 2 subjects

What you’ll study

  • Qur’an

    Students are placed in a Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced Qur’an section based on their skill level.

  • Tafseer

    Translation and explanation of the surahs commonly recited in salah, from Surah al-Bayyinah to Surah an-Naas.

  • Introductory Fiqh, continued

    Further concepts of Islamic law, including Zakah, Hajj, Janaazah and Nikaah.

  • Sahabiyaat (RA)

    The biographies of the wives and daughters of the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).

  • Hadith

    A first hadith text, Zad-ut-Talibeen (Provisions for the Seekers) by Shaykh Ashiq Ilahi Bulandshehri (RH).

  • 99 Names of Allah

    Memorizing the first 50 Names of Allah in Arabic and English.
