Year 2: Tafseer & Hadith

In Year 2, students begin learning and understanding Tafseer (the meaning of the Qur’an) and Hadith, strengthen their Qur’an reading, and continue to deepen their relationship with Allah (SWT).

Year 2 subjects

What you’ll study

Qur’an

Students are placed in a Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced Qur’an section based on their skill level.

Tafseer

Translation and explanation of the surahs commonly recited in salah — Surah al-Bayyinah to Surah an-Naas.

Introductory Fiqh (cont.)

Further concepts of Islamic law, including Zakah, Hajj, Janaazah, and Nikaah.

Sahabiyaat (RA)

The biographies of the wives and daughters of the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).

Hadith

Students begin their first hadith text, Zad-ut-Talibeen (Provisions for the Seekers) by Shaykh Ashiq Ilahi Bulandshehri (RH).

99 Names of Allah

Memorizing the first 50 Names of Allah in Arabic and English.
