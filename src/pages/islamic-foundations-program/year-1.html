Year 1 subjects

What you’ll study

  • Qur’an

    Students are placed in a Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced Qur’an section based on their skill level.

  • ‘Aqeedah

    The beliefs a Muslim holds in the heart with conviction: Allah, His Angels, His Books and the Last Day.

  • Introductory Fiqh

    The first concepts of Islamic law: how to purify oneself (Tahaarah), how to pray salah, and how to fast in Ramadan.

  • Seerah

    The biography of the Prophet Muhammad (SAW), from his birth until his passing.

  • Adab and Akhlaaq

    Islamic manners, and hadith about developing good character.

  • Taarikh

    The stories of the 25 prophets mentioned in the Qur’an.
