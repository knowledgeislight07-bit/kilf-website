Year 1: Foundations of faith

In Year 1, students learn the foundational Islamic concepts they will build on in later years, establish a strong understanding of their Muslim identity, and begin Qur’an and Arabic studies.

Year 1 subjects

What you’ll study

Qur’an

Students are placed in a Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced Qur’an section based on their skill level.

‘Aqeedah

The beliefs a Muslim must hold in the heart with conviction — Allah, His Angels, His Books, and the Last Day.

Introductory Fiqh

The first concepts of Islamic law: how to purify oneself (Tahaarah), how to pray salah, and how to fast in Ramadan.

Seerah

The biography of the Prophet Muhammad (SAW), from his birth until his passing.

Adab & Akhlaaq

Islamic manners, and hadith related to developing good character.

Taarikh

The stories of the 25 prophets mentioned in the Qur’an.
