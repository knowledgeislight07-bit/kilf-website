Knowledge is a light

A strong Islamic foundation, built to last a lifetime

Allah (SWT) tells us in the Qur’an: “If all the trees on earth were pens and the ocean were ink, refilled by seven other oceans, the Words of Allah would not be exhausted. Surely Allah is Almighty, All-Wise.” (Qur’an 31:27)

Islamic scholars have long described beneficial knowledge as a light that Allah places in the heart. It guides a person through every stage of life. Our goal is to give women and girls an avenue to seek that knowledge — and a foundation they can keep building on for a lifetime.

About our foundation
Our mission

Rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah. Built for everyday life.

We cultivate knowledgeable, spiritually grounded Muslims who understand their faith, embody excellent character, and contribute positively to their families and communities.

We strive to

  • Make authentic Islamic education accessible.
  • Build strong foundations in Qur’an and Islamic Studies.
  • Encourage a lifelong relationship with Islamic knowledge.
  • Develop character alongside academic understanding.
  • Prepare students to serve and lead their communities.
  • Create welcoming spaces for Muslims at every stage of their learning journey.

Islamic Foundations Program

A comprehensive, multi-year education in Qur’an and Islamic Studies for women and girls — designed to fit around school, work, and family. Students begin with core beliefs and worship and progress to Tafseer, Hadith, Arabic, and classical Islamic texts.

  • ‘Aqeedah
  • Fiqh
  • Seerah
  • Tafseer
  • Hadith
  • Arabic
  • Adab & Akhlaaq
  • Islamic History
  • Tazkiyah

Online Sisters’ Circles
