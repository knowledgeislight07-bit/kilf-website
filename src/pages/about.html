About Knowledge Is Light Foundation

A Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit providing Islamic education, mentoring and leadership development to Muslim women and girls.

Why “Knowledge Is Light”

We want knowledge to illuminate lives

Islamic scholars have long described beneficial knowledge as a light. It lets a Muslim recognize truth, tell right from wrong, worship Allah correctly, develop good character and make life’s decisions with guidance.

So our goal is not simply to teach information. We want students to build a relationship with Islamic learning that continues long after they leave our classrooms.

Our story

From a few classes in Florida to a foundation

Knowledge Is Light Foundation grew from a commitment to making Islamic education accessible to Muslim students and families. What began as a handful of classes has grown into structured Islamic Studies programs, Qur’an education, leadership development, community classes, youth programming, sisters’ programs and educational partnerships.

KILF became a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit in 2023. Our mission is to provide Islamic education, mentoring and leadership empowerment to women and girls in the United States and neighboring regions, and to help each student live by the Qur’an and Sunnah and understand her worth and identity in Islam.

Helping Muslims learn their Deen, live their Deen, and carry its light into the world.
