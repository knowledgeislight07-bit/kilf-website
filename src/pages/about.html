About Us

A Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to Islamic education, mentoring, and leadership development for Muslim women and girls.

Why “Knowledge Is Light”

We want knowledge to illuminate lives

Islamic scholars have long described beneficial knowledge as a light. Knowledge allows a Muslim to recognize truth, distinguish right from wrong, worship Allah correctly, develop good character, and navigate life’s decisions with guidance.

That’s why our goal is not simply to teach information. We want our students to develop a relationship with Islamic learning that continues long after they leave our classrooms.

Our story

From a few classes to a foundation

Knowledge Is Light Foundation grew from a commitment to making Islamic education accessible to Muslim students and families. What began as a handful of classes in Florida has grown into structured Islamic Studies programs, Qur’an education, leadership development, community classes, youth programming, sisters’ programs, and educational partnerships.

KILF officially became a Florida 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in 2023. Today we continue working toward a simple goal:

Helping Muslims learn their Deen, live their Deen, and carry its light into the world.

Our mission

As the need for Islamic education and mentorship for girls and women grows, Knowledge Is Light Foundation provides Islamic education, mentoring, and leadership empowerment to women and girls in the United States and neighboring regions.

Alongside the core principles of Islam, we mentor and empower our students to live according to the Qur’an and Sunnah and to understand their worth and identity in Islam.
