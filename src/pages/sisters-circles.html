A space to learn, reflect and grow together

Our Online Sisters’ Circles give Muslim women a welcoming, supportive place to come together, strengthen their connection with Allah, and talk about faith and everyday life.

In interactive sessions led by our ‘Alimaat, female Islamic scholars, we explore Islamic teachings, reflect on the Qur’an and Sunnah, and discuss topics that matter to the spiritual and personal growth of Muslim women.

  • Discussion topics and reflections chosen for sisters
  • Support and sisterhood from women on the same path
  • A closer connection with Allah (SWT)
  • No cost, just connection
